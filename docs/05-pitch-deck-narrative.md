**1. Cover** — *Datalith. The Agentic Data Center.* Every source. One graph. Agent-ready. | Raising $4M Seed.

**2. Problem** — Enterprises are deploying AI agents on data that isn't ready for them. Gartner: **>40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027**. Data preparation eats **60–75%** of every agent project. Agents fail quietly: stale data, no semantics, no permissions, no audit trail.

**3. Why now** — Three curves crossing: (1) agent adoption exploding — 17% deployed today, 60%+ within two years; (2) MCP standardized the pipe but not the content — Gartner: 60% of MCP-only projects fail by 2028 for lack of a semantic layer; (3) context is becoming shared infrastructure — 89% of teams investing in the next 12 months (DataHub 2026). *The model war is over for buyers; the context war is starting.*

**4. The two promises** — What clients get, in two beats. **Promise 1 — See everything:** clients have data everywhere and no visibility on what they have or what's happening in their databases; Datalith gives them **visibility, transparency, and control** — a live estate map, an activity stream, and full access control with audit. **Promise 2 — Make it work for agents:** every modality made **agent-ready and agent-friendly**, plus Datalith **detects the potential** sleeping in their DBs and **suggests agentic workflows and knowledge bases** to build. *"First we show you what you have. Then we turn it into an agent workforce."*

**5. Solution** — Datalith connects to every data source, builds the Enterprise Knowledge Graph, scores and prepares data for agents, and serves it — governed — to any agent framework. Read-only to start. Live in 48 hours. *"JetScale did this for cloud spend. We do it for the data estate."*

**6. Product** — The loop: **Connect → Graph → Score → Prepare → Serve → Recommend.** Six modules: Connect (150+ sources, multi-modal), Graph (auto-ontology + identity resolution + lineage), Topics (semantic layer), Ready (prep engine + Agent-Readiness Score), Gateway (governed MCP serving + audit), Flows (recommended agentic workflows with ROI estimates).

**7. Demo / product visual** — Command dashboard: estate map, ARS 0–100 per domain, agents served, recommended workflows queue. (Screenshot/live demo slot.)

**8. The wedge** — Free 48-hour **Agent-Readiness Audit**. Read-only credentials in, board-ready score out. The audit is the lead magnet, benchmark, and land motion in one. Then continuous scoring → graph → gateway → workflows: land free, expand to $100K+ ACV.

### Objection prep (Q&A appendix)
- **"Won't Snowflake/Databricks do this?"** They already do — for their own data. Enterprises average many systems; neutrality is the product (see dbt/Fivetran precedent).
- **"Why not Glean?"** Glean sells answers to employees. We sell infrastructure to agents — open protocols, any framework, governed at the data layer.
- **"Is this a feature of catalogs?"** Catalogs describe data for humans. We prepare and *serve* data to machines, with a runtime (Gateway) and a recommendation engine (Flows). Different consumer, different architecture, different buyer urgency.
- **"MCP churn risk?"** Protocol-agnostic gateway; the knowledge graph is the durable asset.
- **"Why will you win?"** Speed to the category name + the audit wedge generates proprietary readiness benchmark data nobody else has.
