Objection prep

The questions we hope you ask.

01Won’t Snowflake or Databricks build this?+

They already do — for their own data. Enterprises average many systems, and cross-source neutrality is the product. Same durable seat dbt, Fivetran and Confluent occupied against warehouse-native tooling.

02Why not Glean?+

Glean sells answers to employees. We sell infrastructure to agents — open protocols, any framework, governed at the data layer. Different consumer, different architecture, different buyer.

03Isn’t this a feature of data catalogs?+

Catalogs describe data for humans. Datalith prepares and serves data to machines, with a readiness score, a knowledge graph and a use-case recommender. The incumbents’ pivots validate the category; their human-workflow legacy is our opening.

04What about MCP churn risk?+

Serving is protocol-agnostic — MCP today, A2A and OpenAI tool-calling adapters alongside. The durable asset is the knowledge graph, not the wire format.

05Why will Datalith win?+

Speed to the category name, plus the audit wedge: every free audit generates proprietary readiness-benchmark data nobody else has — which compounds into scoring and recommendation quality.
