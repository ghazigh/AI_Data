Company

Data everywhere.
Ready nowhere. Until now.

Enterprises are deploying AI agents faster than their data can support them. Datalith exists to close that gap — to be the layer between the enterprise data estate and the agent workforce. We call the category the Agentic Data Center.

The mission

Make every enterprise’s data ready for its agents.

The model war is over for buyers; the context war is starting. More than 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027, and the autopsy is always the same: the data wasn’t ready. We build the readiness layer — visible, scored, governed, and served to any agent over open protocols.

How we build

Six principles we don't trade away.

Principle 01

Agent-native, human-governed

Agents are the primary consumers; humans set policy, review, and approve.

Principle 02

Read-only by default

Connect with read-only credentials and produce value before asking for anything more. Kills the #1 security objection.

Principle 03

Meet the estate where it is

No migration, no "move your data to us." Federate, index and prepare in place.

Principle 04

Protocol-open

MCP first; adapters for A2A, OpenAI tool calling, and agent SDKs. The graph is the asset; wire formats are commodities.

Principle 05

Score everything

If readiness isn’t measurable, it isn’t manageable. The ARS is the product’s heartbeat.

Principle 06

Ship the wedge first

A free 48-hour audit before a single sales call. The product earns the conversation.
