How the graph's topics are discovered

The graph is not hand-authored. Every structured chunk and typed row across the estate is embedded, projected into a shared coordinate space, clustered, and labelled — the eight knowledge bases fall out of that process rather than being declared in advance.

Adding a dataset to the graph

Nothing here is re-fitted. The projection and the cluster model are the ones fitted once, at construction time, and this path only ever applies them — so a new source lands in the coordinate space that already exists and joins the topics that already exist. An agent bound to Claims yesterday is still bound to Claims today; re-fitting would reshuffle the space and break that binding.
