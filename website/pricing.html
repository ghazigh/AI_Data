Pricing questions

The fine print, out loud.

01Do I have to buy the capabilities separately?+

No — there is no à la carte menu, you pick a plan. Growth runs the graph live and serves your agents; Scale adds workflow recommendations and role-based access; Enterprise adds in-VPC deployment and custom policy.

02What do the usage meters measure?+

Three things: connected sources, graph entities, and Gateway calls. You pay for the estate you connect and the agent traffic you serve — not for seats.

03What happens after the free audit?+

You keep the report — estate map, ARS, top-10 findings. Teams that convert to Growth typically do so to make the score continuous and put their first agents on the Gateway.

04Do you require write access to our systems?+

Never to start. Datalith connects with scoped, read-only credentials, and every read is logged. In-VPC deployment is available on Enterprise.

05How do contracts work?+

Growth and Scale are monthly, cancel anytime. Enterprise is annual with a blended target ACV of $60K in year one — customers typically reach $90K+ by year two as usage expands.
