Questions

Asked by every data leader.

01Is access really read-only?+

Yes. Datalith ingests through scoped, read-only credentials; nothing writes back to your systems, and every read is logged in the audit trail.

02What is the Agent-Readiness Score?+

ARS is a 0–100 measure of how consumable your estate is for AI agents — coverage, semantics, governance and freshness, computed per domain and overall.

03How is this different from a data catalog?+

Catalogs describe data for humans. Datalith produces governed, agent-ready context and serves it over MCP — with per-agent identity and full audit.

04How long does the audit take?+

48 hours from credentials to a board-ready readout: estate map, ARS, and the top opportunities sleeping in your data.
