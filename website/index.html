Questions

Asked by every data leader.

01What exactly am I buying?+

One platform with six capabilities: connection, the knowledge graph, business definitions, preparation and scoring, governed serving, and workflow recommendations. You pick a plan rather than buying capabilities à la carte — higher plans connect more sources, serve more agent traffic, and add deeper governance and workflow recommendations.

02How is this different from a data catalog, or from Glean?+

A catalog describes data for people to find. Glean answers employee questions. Datalith prepares data for machines and serves it to agents through a governed endpoint, with per-agent identity and a full audit trail.

03Is access really read-only?+

Yes. Datalith connects with scoped, read-only credentials; nothing writes back to your systems, and every read is logged in the audit trail.

04What is the Agent-Readiness Score?+

A 0–100 measure of how usable your data is for AI agents — how much of the estate is covered, how well it is defined, how well it is governed, and how fresh it is. Computed per business domain and estate-wide.

05How long does the audit take?+

48 hours from credentials to a board-ready readout: your estate map, your score, and the top opportunities sleeping in your data.
