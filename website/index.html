The agentic platform

What the Datalith platform gives you.

Five stages, in order — from the systems you already run to the agents your data makes possible.

Stage 01 · Connect

Plugs into every database you run.

Warehouses, OLTP databases, SaaS apps, object stores, streams — connected with scoped read-only credentials, in an afternoon.

No migration and no engineering project. Nothing is ever written back to your systems.

Stage 02 · Ready

Scores how ready your data is for agents.

Every source and every business domain gets an Agent-Readiness Score out of 100 — coverage, definitions, governance, freshness — and Datalith keeps watching it after the first pass.

Continuous monitoring, not a one-off audit. You see readiness move as your estate changes.

Console shows an illustrative estate. Datalith is pre-launch; these are not customer figures.

Stage 03 · Pipelines

Proposes the restructuring your estate actually needs.

Based on what the score found, Datalith designs the pipelines that take your data from the state it is in today to a shape agents can consume — deduplicated, typed, chunked, masked.

Built for your data as it is, not a generic template someone else's estate needed.

Stage 04 · Graph

Builds one map of your whole business.

Every system resolved into a single knowledge graph, with the topics, domains and pockets of expertise inside it detected and named — so each becomes a knowledge base in its own right.

Knowledge bases any agentic AI can read, whatever framework you build on.

Stage 05 · Use cases

Tells you which agents are worth building.

Datalith reads the graph and proposes the agentic applications your data actually supports — what each one would need, and what it would be worth.

You find out what to build before you spend a quarter building it.

Example workflows and payback figures are modelled, not customer results. Datalith is pre-launch.

Questions

Asked by every data leader.

01What exactly am I buying?+

One platform with six capabilities: connection, the knowledge graph, business definitions, preparation and scoring, governed serving, and workflow recommendations. You pick a plan rather than buying capabilities à la carte — higher plans connect more sources, serve more agent traffic, and add deeper governance and workflow recommendations.

02How is this different from a data catalog, or from Glean?+

A catalog describes data for people to find. Glean answers employee questions. Datalith prepares data for machines and serves it to agents through a governed endpoint, with per-agent identity and a full audit trail.

03Is access really read-only?+

Yes. Datalith connects with scoped, read-only credentials; nothing writes back to your systems, and every read is logged in the audit trail.

04What is the Agent-Readiness Score?+

A 0–100 measure of how usable your data is for AI agents — how much of the estate is covered, how well it is defined, how well it is governed, and how fresh it is. Computed per business domain and estate-wide.

05How long does the audit take?+

48 hours from credentials to a board-ready readout: your estate map, your score, and the top opportunities sleeping in your data.
