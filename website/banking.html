FAQ

Questions banks ask.

01Do you need write access to our core systems?+

No. Datalith connects with scoped, read-only credentials and never writes back to a system of record. Agents read prepared context from the graph; any action they propose stays inside your own workflow tools, under your own approvals.

02Can it run inside our own perimeter?+

Yes. SaaS in the region you choose, or deployed entirely inside your own cloud account. Datalith federates and prepares in place — only what serving requires is ever materialised.

03How do agents respect who is allowed to see what?+

Entitlements are read from the source systems at ingest and enforced at query time, so an agent sees only what the person or service behind it could already see. Customer, book and region restrictions travel with the data instead of being rebuilt.

04What happens to personal data?+

It is detected and masked or tokenised at ingest, before anything is served. The graph serves references rather than raw identifiers unless your policy says otherwise, and every read is logged against the identity that asked.

05Most of our answers live in documents, not tables. Does that work?+

That is the point. Policies, contracts, credit files, correspondence and transcripts each get the extraction their format needs and land as typed records with citations back to the source page, so a document-bound answer can still be checked.

06How does this survive an internal review?+

Datalith is not a model — it is the data layer underneath one. What it gives a reviewer is the trail: which sources an answer drew on, how fresh they were, who was cleared to see them, and what was read, by which agent, on whose behalf, and when.

07What do you need from us to start?+

Read-only credentials on three sources, roughly two hours of a data engineer’s time to issue them, and 45 minutes at the end for the readout. No migration, no software installed in your estate, no commitment.
