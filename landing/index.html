FAQ

Questions VCs and CISOs ask us.

How is this different from a data catalog like Atlan or Alation?

Catalogs describe data for human stewards. Datalith prepares and serves data to machines: a knowledge graph with a governed runtime (Gateway), a preparation engine, and workflow recommendations. Different consumer, different architecture, different urgency.

Why isn't MCP alone enough?

MCP moves context; it doesn't produce it. Gartner projects 60% of agentic analytics projects that rely solely on MCP will fail by 2028 for lack of a consistent semantic layer. Datalith is that layer — plus preparation, governance, and serving.

Do we have to move our data?

No. Datalith connects read-only, indexes and prepares in place, and materializes only what serving requires. No migration, no new system of record.

Which agent frameworks are supported?

Anything that speaks MCP works out of the box; we also ship adapters and SDKs for LangGraph, CrewAI, the OpenAI Agents SDK, and n8n. The graph is the asset — protocols are just wire formats.

How fast is time-to-value?

48 hours from read-only credentials to your first Agent-Readiness Score and estate map. Most teams connect their first four sources in a single afternoon.
